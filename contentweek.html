Monday · Blog post

More than half of Ramp's businesses now pay for AI. Here's what that number does and doesn't mean.

In October 2026, 56.8% of U.S. businesses on Ramp paid for an AI tool C1, up 13.3 points in a year C2. That's a real shift, but it describes Ramp's customers, which Ramp itself calls more tech-forward. The Census Bureau's survey puts AI use at 22% of firms C4. The index also counts only paid transactions, so a team using a free chatbot is invisible to it C5. And the climb is flattening: just one point from September to October C3. The takeaway: paying for AI is becoming normal among tech-forward businesses, and the easy growth may be over.
