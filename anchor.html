Concept: equivalent fractions (Grade 4). Core idea: multiplying the top and bottom by the same number names the same amount in smaller pieces. Use: printed on chart paper at the front of the room.

Equivalent Fractions

Same amount. Different-sized pieces.

Multiply top and bottom

1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8
×2 on top, ×2 on bottom.
The pieces get smaller, and you get more of them.

Check it with a picture

The shaded part is the same size.

⚠️ Adding the same number does not work: 1/2 ≠ 2/3

Infographic: Is it equivalent? 3 steps

1Find what the bottom was multiplied by. 3/4 → ?/12: 4 × 3 = 12.
2Multiply the top by the same number: 3 × 3 = 9.
3Check with a picture or by dividing: 9/12 and 3/4 both equal 0.75. ✅
